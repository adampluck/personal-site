orbidal.xyz & refund.fi

A fairer penny auction, on-chain. Bids fund the prize first, then the surplus goes back to the people who didn't win. Orbidal is the space-themed brand with stock tokens as prizes. refund.fi runs crypto prizes on the same contract.

one contract, two brands, anyone can run the bots

How it runs

  1. Bid 50¢ to add 1¢ to the price and reset a 15-second clock
  2. Settle in a smart contract that holds every bid. No back office
  3. Keep things moving with bots that close auctions and fire auto-bids
  4. Rebate the surplus to losing bidders, and fund public goods

aluba.xyz

A lowest unique bid auction: the smallest bid nobody else matched wins. Bids stay sealed until the round ends, so nobody can peek, including me.

even the explainer video is rendered from code

How it runs

  1. Verify you're a real, unique adult with ZKPassport, sharing nothing else
  2. Seal each bid, time-locked to a future drand round
  3. Reveal the bids when the round ends, and settle automatically
  4. Prove the results with a zero-knowledge proof anyone can check
